~ official support department (real) ~

welcome to the NedBot official support department. this is a real system. your issues are logged into a real database running on real hardware in a real data centre in amsterdam or possibly frankfurt, i have not checked recently.

what happens to your issue: it is received. it is assigned a reference number. it is stored. ned reviews tickets when he has time, which is sometimes never, but sometimes sooner. no timeline is guaranteed. no fix is guaranteed. the logging is guaranteed. the logging is the most reliable part of this process.

issues that will receive the most attention: things that are actually broken. issues where you can explain what you did and what happened. issues that include the phrase "i tried this and it did not work" followed by what you tried.
issues that will receive less attention: "it's broken" with no further detail. feature requests that require ned to learn a new programming language. anything submitted more than three times. anything that is swimmernick's fault (he has his own process).
