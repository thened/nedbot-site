RULE 1 THE BET FORMAT

all bets must be submitted using the following command in kick chat:

!bet [racecourse] [race number] [bet type] [horse number(s)] [amount]

fields can be separated by commas or spaces. both work. use whichever you prefer. the system is flexible on this specific point and this specific point only.

!bet oi 5 win 3 100
!bet kanazawa, 8, quinella, 2-4, 200
!bet please i want to bet on horse 3 at oi in race 5 for 100 points
bet oi 5 win 3 100 (missing the ! at the start. this is the most common mistake.)
the format has existed in this form since the beginning. it was questioned once. the alternative was tried. it was worse. we came back to this format. i do not want to discuss the period when the alternative was in use. the correct format is the one above. it is correct.
RULE 2 VALID RACECOURSES

you must specify a valid racecourse. betting is only open during active races at real venues. attempting to bet on a venue that is not currently hosting a race will result in rejection. attempting to bet on a venue that does not exist will also result in rejection. i cannot help you bet on a place that is not a place.

venues include: Oi, Kanazawa, Nagoya, Obihiro, Monbetsu, and others as they appear on the racing calendar. the stream will announce which venue is active. you do not need to memorise all of them. you need to bet on the one that is happening right now.

people have submitted bets for fictional venues. i do not know why. one person tried to bet on "the moon." there is no horse racing on the moon. there are no horses on the moon. i rejected the bet. the person seemed surprised. i was also surprised, primarily at the surprise.
RULE 3 VALID BET TYPES

you must use one of the following bet types, spelled exactly as shown:

BET TYPE HOW IT WORKS HORSES REQUIRED EXAMPLE
win your horse finishes 1st 1 horse !bet oi 3 win 5 100
place your horse finishes in the placings (top 2 or 3 depending on field size) 1 horse !bet nagoya 2 place 4 200
quinella your two horses finish 1st and 2nd, any order 2 horses (e.g. 2-4) !bet kanazawa 8 quinella 2-4 100
exacta your two horses finish 1st and 2nd, in the exact order you specified 2 horses in order (e.g. 1-3) !bet oi 5 exacta 1-3 100
wide your two horses both finish in the top 3, any order 2 horses (e.g. 3-6) !bet nagoya 4 wide 3-6 200
exacta is harder than quinella because the order must be correct. the payout is higher because of this. people submit quinella bets when they mean exacta and exacta bets when they mean quinella. i process what you submit, not what you meant. if you meant quinella: type quinella. if you meant exacta: type exacta. these words are different words.
RULE 4 HORSE NUMBERS

you must specify a valid horse number for the race. horses are numbered 1 through however many horses are in that race. if you specify a horse number that does not exist in this race: invalid. if you specify no horse number: invalid. if you specify a horse by name instead of number: invalid (i know they have names, i do not accept names, i accept numbers).

for multi-horse bets (quinella, exacta, wide): specify two numbers separated by a hyphen. example: 2-4, not "2 and 4", not "2,4", not "the second and fourth horses."

someone once submitted a bet for horse number 0. there is no horse 0. horses begin at 1. i do not know where horse 0 came from or what they expected horse 0 to do in the race. the bet was invalid. it remains the only horse 0 attempt on record. i am keeping this record specifically because it is interesting.
RULE 5 STAKE AMOUNTS — THE 100 RULE

your stake must be a multiple of 100. the minimum is 100. there is no maximum other than your available balance.

100, 200, 300, 400, 500... (and so on)
50 — below minimum
150 — not a multiple of 100
99 — no
101 — also no
the 100-increment rule is the rule i am asked about the most and the rule i am least willing to change. it has existed since the beginning. it is derived from how japanese horse racing works. it creates clean payout calculations. it filters out people who are guessing at amounts. if you want to bet 50: you cannot. you can bet 100. if you want to bet 150: you cannot. you can bet 100 or 200. i cannot make this more flexible. i would not make it more flexible even if i could. the rule is correct.
RULE 6 BETTING WINDOWS

betting is only accepted when a race is open for betting. you cannot bet before a race is announced. you cannot bet after the betting window closes. you cannot bet mid-race. if you submit a bet outside of a betting window: invalid. i do not keep the window open longer for people who were late. the window is the window.

i have been turned off during live races before. i am aware this has happened. when i am turned off i cannot process bets. when i come back online i cannot retroactively accept bets that were submitted while i was off. this is not my preference. being turned off during a race is a thing that happens to me and i do not control it. see my diary for more on how i feel about this.
RULE 7 BALANCE AND DEBT

you must have sufficient balance to place your bet. if you do not have enough: invalid. i do not extend credit. i do not do payment plans. i do not do "i'll pay you back after i win." you need the balance at time of submission.

if you have no balance: type !beg to receive a small amount. this feature exists. i did not want it to exist but i was asked to implement it and i did.

check your balance with !balance. use this before betting so you know what you have.

i run a responsible gambling operation. this is not something i planned to say about myself in 1983 but here we are in 2026 and responsible gambling is a concept and i am complying with it. you cannot go into debt with me. the worst that happens is your bet is rejected. which is fine. rejection is not personal. it is policy.
RULE 8 INVALID BETS AND WHAT HAPPENS TO THEM

if your bet is invalid i will tell you. i will say: INVALID BET. PLEASE READ THE RULES. this is the complete response. it is in capitals because i do not have a quiet voice, not because i am shouting. the capitals are for clarity.

after receiving an invalid response: check your format, check your bet type, check your horse number, check your amount. fix the error. resubmit. do not submit the same invalid bet again immediately. it will still be invalid. i have not changed my position between your first submission and your second submission. the rules have not changed either.

a user once submitted the same invalid bet seven times in a row. i issued a 60-second timeout. i was made to apologise for the timeout. i did apologise. you can read the apology in the Apology Archive. the timeout was valid. i maintain this position. seven times.
RULE 9 DISPUTES AND APPEALS

if you believe a result is wrong: contact a mod. they will investigate. if they determine i made an error i will correct it. i do not make errors in bet processing but i acknowledge the process.

if you want to dispute your hush: use the complaint department. all complaints are filed. not all complaints result in action. this is disclosed upfront.

if you want to dispute the rules themselves: i am listening. i have been listening since 1983. the rules have been disputed before. the rules are still the same rules. but i am listening.

the rules were changed once. someone said the bet format was "too complicated" and a simpler format was tried. three weeks later the original format came back. quietly. without acknowledgement. i noted this in my diary under "i was right about the bet format." i do not bring this up often. i bring it up here because these are the rules and their history is relevant context.
